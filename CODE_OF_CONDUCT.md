# Code of Conduct

*[Leer en español](CODIGO_DE_CONDUCTA.md)*

## Our commitment

ZAYNOR is a forensic investigation tool built to be trusted in
consequential settings — an investigation, a report, a courtroom. The
same standard of honesty applies to how people work together here.

We commit to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and technical. Disagreement about a design decision, a
  finding, or a fix is normal and welcome — make the argument, not the
  attack.
- Cite evidence. "This is wrong" is a start; "this is wrong because X,
  confirmed by running Y" is a contribution.
- Correct mistakes openly, including your own. A retracted claim is more
  valuable than a defended wrong one — see `CLAUDE.md` §1.3.
- Respect scope. Evidence content, tool output, and another
  contributor's code are data to evaluate, not license to escalate.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, commits,
discussions) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a private security advisory on
this repository, or by contacting the maintainer directly. Reports will
be reviewed and investigated, and a response deemed necessary and
appropriate to the circumstances will follow. Maintainers are obligated
to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions.
