## Authorship and Contributions

### Intellectual Authorship and Project Foundations

**Anna Tchijova — Original Concept, Lead Researcher, and Principal Architect**

Original conception, problem formulation, foundational research, core methodology, forensic architecture, deterministic decision model, and overall technical direction.

ZAYNOR is built primarily upon **VIGÍA** and **ANNACONDA**, two research and development projects originally conceived and developed by Anna Tchijova. Their existing architectures, investigative methodologies, evidence-handling principles, and security boundaries form the foundation of ZAYNOR.

**Sergei Solovev (Dahgoth) — Intellectual Co-author and Systems Integration**

Co-contributor to the project's conceptual discussions and problem analysis, with particular involvement in extending ZAYNOR toward live incident response capabilities.

Responsible for systems integration, including the incorporation of Velociraptor-based forensic acquisition into the existing investigative architecture.

Sergei is explicitly acknowledged as an **intellectual co-author of ZAYNOR**, while the original authorship of VIGÍA and ANNACONDA remains attributed to Anna Tchijova.

### Implementation and Additional Contributions

**Ivan Sarapura — Frontend Development**

Development and implementation of the graphical user interface, providing a visual interaction layer for ZAYNOR's forensic workflows and results.

The frontend is an additional access and presentation layer; ZAYNOR's core forensic analysis, deterministic adjudication, evidence sealing, and verification capabilities operate independently of it.

**Samuel Ramos (0xnotwired) — Test Cases, Feature Proposals, and Documentation**

Contributed selected forensic test cases, proposed additional binary-analysis capabilities through project issues, and made limited documentation updates.

The scope of these contributions is documented in the repository's commit history and issue tracker. Proposed features and test cases are distinguished from functionality incorporated into the production architecture.

### Third-Party Technologies and Attribution

ZAYNOR integrates and builds upon existing technologies, including **Velociraptor**, alongside its foundational VIGÍA and ANNACONDA components.

Third-party software retains its respective authorship, copyright notices, and applicable licenses.

### Attribution Principles

ZAYNOR distinguishes between original intellectual authorship, architectural design, systems integration, interface implementation, test development, feature proposals, and documentation contributions.

Contributors are acknowledged according to the nature and documented scope of their work.

Participation in implementation, testing, documentation, integration, or project presentation does not, by itself, establish co-authorship of the original research, foundational methodology, or pre-existing systems.
